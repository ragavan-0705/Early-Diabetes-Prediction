What is Diabetes?

Diabetes is a chronic disease that affects how your body turns food into energy. It leads to high blood sugar levels and can cause serious health complications.

Causes of Diabetes

  • Genetic factors
  • Unhealthy diet
  • Obesity
  • Lack of physical activity
  • Insulin resistance

Common Symptoms

  • Frequent urination
  • Excessive thirst
  • Fatigue
  • Blurred vision
  • Unexplained weight loss

Diabetes Prediction

Body Mass Index. Normal: 18-25, Overweight: 25-30, Obese: 30+
Average blood sugar over 3 months (%). Normal: <5.7, Prediabetes: 5.7-6.4, Diabetes: ≥6.5
Current blood sugar (mg/dL). Normal fasting: <100, Prediabetes: 100-125, Diabetes: ≥126
High blood pressure (140/90 mmHg or higher)
Includes heart attack, coronary artery disease, or angina
